WebMusic is a toolkit for music software, interactive demos, and web-based
musical installations. Use ready-made **Web Components**, drive **Headless
objects** with your own interface, or compose **APIs and UI presenters**
directly. These choices share data and runtime contracts so a prototype can
grow without replacing every layer.

Online music workstations and expressive installations are long-term
application goals. Project models, undo, persistence, and collaboration remain
application responsibilities to design on top of the toolkit.

The first release focuses on symbolic music and its shared foundations:

| Package | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [`@webmusic/score`](/score/) | Symbolic music — MIDI, MusicXML, MXL, ABC: playback, views, analysis. |
| [`@webmusic/ui`](/uikit/) | Dependency-free DOM presenters and styling hooks for Score or application-owned behavior. |
| [`@webmusic/kernel`](/kernel/) | Domain-neutral runtime contracts, lifecycle helpers and timing logic. |

**Web Components** document the `/element` form, **Headless** documents
`/headless`, **API** documents the package root and its capability subpaths,
and **UI Kit** documents `@webmusic/ui`. Score works with notation and note
events, including sound synthesis and offline WAV export.

Audio-file processing and cross-domain Bridge coordination are accepted future
directions. Their packages and public reference pages are absent from this
first-release checkout.

Start with **[Quick Start](/quick-start/)** for one player and its controls.
The [Score page](/score/) covers installation and registration, and
routes to playback, analysis, views and custom interfaces.
