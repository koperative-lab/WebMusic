Third-party code embedded in OpenSheetMusicDisplay 1.9.9

The official release source map identifies JSZip, loglevel, TypeScript
Collections and patched VexFlow. JSZip itself includes immediate, lie, pako
and setimmediate. Its browser stream entry is an external placeholder, so
readable-stream and its Node dependencies are not included in this record.
JSZip is used under the MIT option. Pako also retains its zlib source notice.
